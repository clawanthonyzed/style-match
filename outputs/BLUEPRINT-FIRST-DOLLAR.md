## 🎯 1. Exact Offer

**"Find Your Colour Season": self-test guide + 4 palette cards (PDF)**

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| 📖 Self-test | 10-minute guided test at home: natural light, white vs cream fabric, gold vs silver jewellery, vein check. Decision tree → your season. |
| 🎨 4 season palettes | Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter. 24 swatches each with hex codes, "wear this, skip that" lists. |
| 🛍️ Shopping cheat sheet | One page per season: best neutrals, denim wash, lipstick families, metals |

Self-serve, so no one has to analyse a photo. It sells the result of a A$200+ stylist session as a A$19 product. A personalised photo report (A$49) is the upsell **after** the first sales, not before.

## ✅ 3. Setup Checklist (5 steps)

1. **Write the guide (Days 1-2):** Iris writes the test, decision tree, and 4 palettes. Use real colour theory language, not vague "warm vibes".
2. **Lay it out (Day 3):** a simple, clean PDF with reportlab or a free Canva account. 12-16 pages. Swatches are just coloured squares, no image generation needed.
3. **Free quiz (Day 4):** a 6-question Tally.so quiz (free tier) that gives a "likely season" and links to the full guide. This is the email-capture funnel.
4. **Gumroad listing (Day 5):** A$0 upfront. 4 cover images (one per season). Description leads with the outcome: "Stop buying clothes that wash you out."
5. **Vibe QC (Day 5):** 9.5/10 minimum before it goes live.

## 🏆 5. 10/10 Quality Rubric

| Must-have | Why it beats the competition |
|---|---|
| 🧪 A test you can actually do alone at home | Most guides just describe the seasons and leave the buyer guessing |
| #️⃣ Hex codes on every swatch | Buyers can match them in shopping apps. Most Etsy palettes are pictures only. |
| 🇦🇺 Australian shopping examples | Uniqlo, Country Road, Kmart, not US-only brands |
| 🚫 "Skip these" lists | People value what to stop buying as much as what to buy |
| 🔍 Vibe QC pass at 9.5/10+ | Empire minimum |
