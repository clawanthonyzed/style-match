# StyleMatch — Business Plan
*Created: 2026-04-26 | Manager: Sienna*

## Concept
Personal colour analysis + AI-powered shopping that only shows you colours that suit you.

## Problem
Women spend hours shopping online and return items because "it just doesn't look right."
The reason is usually wrong colour — they haven't had a colour analysis.
Professional colour analysis costs $200-$500 AUD. Apps that do it are US-focused and don't connect to Australian retailers.

## Solution
StyleMatch: free colour analysis → personalised shopping feed showing only colours that work for you.

## Go-To-Market
1. TikTok and Instagram: colour analysis content (huge trend in 2024-2025)
2. Sienna/zara-marin audience cross-promotion
3. SEO: "personal colour analysis Australia", "what colours suit me"
4. Influencer partnerships: Australian fashion influencers
